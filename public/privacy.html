Nakshatra Astro-AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-08

Nakshatra Astro-AI ("the app") is a night-sky astrophotography tool. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

Camera

The app uses your device camera to capture long-exposure and burst photo sequences for stacking. Captured frames are processed on-device. Final stacked images you choose to save are stored locally on your device (and, if you enable Auto-Save, in the app's local gallery database) — they are not uploaded to any server by default, except when you explicitly request AI analysis (see below).

Location (GPS)

The app uses your device's location to compute star positions, sky visibility, and light-pollution estimates for your current position. Location data is used locally for these calculations and is sent to our analysis server only alongside an image you submit for AI identification, to improve the accuracy of that analysis. Location is never sold or shared with third parties.

AI Image Analysis

When you request AI identification of a captured image, the image, your approximate location, and device orientation are sent to our backend server, which forwards them to NVIDIA's NIM API for analysis. This is optional — analysis only happens when you initiate a capture session.

Ads

The app displays ads via Google AdMob to support free use. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and use them for ad personalization and measurement, subject to Google's own privacy policy. You can remove all ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.

Payments

The one-time ad-free unlock is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Google handles payment collection directly through your Google account, and the app only receives confirmation that the purchase succeeded in order to unlock the ad-free feature on your device.

Data Storage

Your photo gallery, session notes, and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this local data. The ad-free unlock status is also stored locally.

Third-Party Services

The app uses free third-party APIs for weather (Open-Meteo), ISS tracking (Open Notify), and NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day — these requests do not include personally identifying information beyond your location coordinates when relevant.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer at the email listed on the Play Store listing for this app.
